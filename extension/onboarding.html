Tabs2Notion

Send open tabs straight to Notion.

No server, API key, or developer setup. Tabs2Notion connects directly to Notion using Notion MCP OAuth with PKCE.

1. Connect Notion

Notion opens its own authorization window. Tabs2Notion never receives your Notion password.

2. Use it

Click the Tabs2Notion toolbar icon, or use the right-click menu. Choose the tabs you want to send, then select a connected workspace and search for the destination Notion database.

Help

Privacy

Tab titles and URLs are sent only to the Notion workspace/database you choose. OAuth credentials are encrypted locally in this Chrome profile and are not sent to a Tabs2Notion server.
